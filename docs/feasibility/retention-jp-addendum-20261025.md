## Additional official evidence

The [Personal Information Protection Commission FAQ Q5-2](https://www.ppc.go.jp/all_faq_index/faq1-q5-2/) states no universal personal-information storage period.
It explains the effort required to erase personal data without delay after its use becomes unnecessary.
The [general guidelines, section 3-4-1](https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/guidelines_tsusoku/) connect storage-period settings to the specific processing purpose.

The [PayPal Japan User Agreement](https://www.paypal.com/jp/legalhub/paypal/useragreement-full?locale.x=en_JP) was updated on May 28, 2026.
Its chargeback section assigns card disputes to the card issuer.
Its legal-process section permits some account holds beyond 180 days.
Account balance holds do not establish an image retention requirement.
These statements do not establish a universal final date for PACT image deletion.
